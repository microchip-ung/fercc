# Contributing to fercc

Thank you for your interest in fercc. Before you open a pull request or send us
a patch, please take a moment to read this document - it explains how fercc is
developed and, importantly, why we handle contributions differently from a
typical community-driven open-source project.

## fercc is open source, but not a community project

fercc is released under the [MIT license](LICENSE), so you are free to use,
modify, and redistribute it within the terms of that license. We are genuinely
glad the source is useful to you, and we actively welcome forks. If fercc does
not quite fit your needs, forking it and adapting it is exactly the right thing
to do - the MIT license places no restrictions on that.

However, fercc is **not** a community-developed project. It is developed and
maintained by Microchip Technology Inc., which holds full copyright over the
codebase and intends to keep it that way. Every change is developed against
Microchip's internal engineering, quality, and release processes, which are not
visible in this public repository.

For that reason, **we do not accept contributions through GitHub.** Pull
requests are disabled, and GitHub Issues are not monitored. We also do not
accept patches or bug reports by email, and we do not provide technical support
through email or GitHub.

This is a deliberate choice, not an oversight. Please read on for how to reach
us, and for what to do if you want to send us code to explain a problem.

## How to reach us

Whether you have found a bug, need a feature, have a question, or want to
propose a change, the right path is the same:

- **Microchip Support Portal:** <https://support.microchip.com/>
- Your regional **Sales**, **Marketing**, **Applications (Apps)**, or
  **Field Application Engineer (FAE)** contacts

These channels are backed by our engineering teams, are tracked, and are the
only supported way to get issues in front of the people who maintain fercc.
Reports filed here reach us reliably; issues filed on GitHub do not.

## Reporting security vulnerabilities

**Please do not report security issues through public GitHub issues, pull
requests, or any public forum.**

Suspected security vulnerabilities should be reported to Microchip's Product
Security Incident Response Team (PSIRT). See Microchip's security reporting
page for the current process and contact details:

- <https://www.microchip.com/psirt>

Responsible disclosure gives us the opportunity to investigate and address the
issue before it is made public, which protects users of fercc and the products
built on it.

## Sending code to explain a problem

Sending us code is welcome when it helps explain something. A diff, a stack
trace, or a small reproduction is often the clearest possible way to show us
what you mean, and we would much rather see a concrete example than guess at it.
If attaching some code to a support case makes a bug easier to understand or a
proposal easier to evaluate, please do.

Please understand, though, that **we do not incorporate customer code into
fercc.** We do not merge patches, and anything you send is used only to help us
understand and reproduce the issue you are raising. If we decide a change is
warranted, it is designed and implemented by Microchip through our own
engineering process and released as a normal fercc change - it is not taken from
your submission.

This is deliberate, and it protects you as well: sending code to illustrate a
point requires no agreement, no paperwork, and no transfer of rights on your
part. You keep your code, and fercc stays entirely Microchip-developed, which
lets us maintain a single, clean copyright and continue to steward the license.

### In short

If your goal is to get a bug fixed or a feature added, **contact Microchip
support or your Microchip field contacts.** Attach code whenever it helps
explain the problem - that is genuinely useful and asks nothing of you legally.
Just note that we will not merge it: any change we make to fercc is Microchip's
own independent work.

We appreciate you taking the time to understand how fercc is maintained, and we
look forward to hearing from you through the channels above.
